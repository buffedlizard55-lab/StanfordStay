## 🏨 Base facts (verified)

- **Stanford Hotel Myeongdong** — 84 Namdaemun-ro (Namdaemun-ro 2-ga 9-1), Jung-gu. Official site: stanfordmyeongdong.com · ☎ +82-2-6260-2000. **Check-in 15:00 / check-out 12:00.**
- **Station access:** ~2 min walk **Euljiro 1-ga (Line 2, Exit 6)** · ~8 min walk **Myeongdong (Line 4)** · ~10 min walk **Jonggak (Line 1)**. Airport bus 6015 stops at Myeongdong Kookmin Bank nearby. *(Hotel listing + booking listings, Aug 20, 2026)*
- Because the hotel sits at the **north edge of Myeong-dong on Line 2**, Line 2's inner loop (DDP → Hongdae → City Hall) is your backbone; Line 4 from Myeongdong Station covers Seoul Station–Ichon (National Museum) southbound and Changgyeonggung/Hyehwa northbound.

## 🚇 Transit notes

- All door-to-door times in these files are **estimates (≈)** for this hotel — confirm live routes in **Naver Map or Kakao Map** (Google Maps is unreliable in Korea; per KoreaFun `travel-basics.md`).
- **T-money card** (any convenience store, ~₩3,000–4,000 + load) or **WOWPASS** (transit + prepaid KRW card, kiosks at ICN). Short-term **Seoul Climate Card**: 1-day ₩5,000 · 2-day ₩8,000 · 3-day ₩10,000 · 5-day ₩15,000 · 7-day ₩20,000 — Seoul-only transit; **does not cover the Incheon Airport AREX stretch** (Gimpo–Seoul Station only). *(travel-basics.md, fares verified Aug 18, 2026)*
- **⚠️ Sun Nov 1 morning:** the **JTBC Seoul Marathon (08:00 start, ~32,000 runners)** closes central-Seoul roads (Sangam→Mapo Bridge→Yeouido→Gangnam→Jamsil) until early afternoon. **Use the subway, not taxis/buses, before ~14:00 that day.** *(KoreaFun seoul.md #2)*

## ✅ Verification standard (same rule the source repos use)

- **🔎 VERIFIED PLACE** — the place exists on an official page with hours; ordinary changes still possible.
- **⏳ RE-CHECK** — real program, but its Nov 2026 schedule is unpublished (checked Aug 20, 2026: **Seoul Kimjang Culture Festival 2026 dates are still unpublished** — plan around it, not on it).
