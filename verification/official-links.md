## 6. How this directory was built & how to use it

- **Every activity link** comes from the KoreaFun repo's own "Official source(s)" lines (which were protocol-verified Aug 17–19, 2026), and every **restaurant/café link** from the Koreafood verified roster (Visit Seoul / VisitKorea / Michelin pages only — no prices are printed there or here).
- **The 23 rows in §1 were fetched live today (Aug 21, 2026)** and matched the itineraries exactly. Nothing enters an itinerary unless it traces to one of these pages.
- **Hours change.** Korean businesses adjust hours and closing days; re-open the linked page (or check Naver Map on the day) for each stop. The [booking checklist](booking-checklist.md) lists what to re-verify in mid-October.
- **Booking rule of thumb:** ticketed shows (La Bohème, Elisabeth, Glass Menagerie, Jujutsu Kaisen, Regallily, Gugak) and BANKSY book first; KGMA ticketing opens ~October — watch [kgma-is.com](https://kgma-is.com/locales/en/index.html); Huwon opens [6 days ahead at 10:00](https://www.cdg.go.kr); Mulbit Yeonhwa needs no booking.
