# 🏯 Plan F — Heritage & Palaces (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** one royal / museum / temple cluster per day, sequenced off the palace-closure matrix. Food is the nearest verified table, not a second destination. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Palace matrix (KoreaFun seoul #29–37):** Gyeongbokgung + Jongmyo + Blue House **closed Tue (Nov 3)** · Changdeokgung + Deoksugung + Changgyeonggung **closed Mon (Nov 2, 9)** · Deoksugung uniquely open **09:00–21:00**.

| Date | Day / pace | Heritage cluster | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nov 1** | Sun 🟩 | **Seoul Plaza / Gwanghwamun + Myeong-dong** | [Marathon](https://en.marathon.jtbc.com/) AM — subway only until ~14:00. Check-in 15:00 → ⭐ **Seoul [Outdoor Library](https://festival.seoul.go.kr/festival/main/festivalView.do?festacode=394) last day** (Fri–Sun; night session **16:00–22:00** at Seoul Plaza / Gwanghwamun Book Plaza — walk from hotel) → cathedral → **[NANTA](https://www.nanta.co.kr/kr/show/detail.php?id=1) 17:00**. |
| **Nov 2** | Mon 🟥 | **[Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr)** (the Monday-open palace) | **J1.** 10:00 royal guard ceremony → [Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) + **National Palace Museum** + **National Folk Museum** (both open Mon) → lunch **Hwangsaengga** → [Bukchon](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr) exterior walk (Baek Inje is Tue–Sun — skip the house or save it) → [Insadong](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/1270460). **[Changdeokgung](https://www.cdg.go.kr) / [Deoksugung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) / Changgyeonggung / most city museums closed Mon.** |
| **Nov 3** | Tue 🟥 | **[Changdeokgung + Huwon](https://www.cdg.go.kr)** (the Tuesday-open palace) | **J2.** [Huwon](https://www.cdg.go.kr) slot booked 6 days out (alarm **Tue Oct 28, 10:00**, [cdg.go.kr](https://www.cdg.go.kr)). Palace + Secret Garden (peak foliage) → **Seoul Museum of Korean Folk Music** opposite the gate (Tue–Sun, free, 《다시, 아리랑》 to May 2027) → Seochon **[Dilkusha](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/www/intro/annexIntro/annex_dilkusha/annex_dilkusha_01.jsp)** (1923) + Tongin. **[Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) / Jongmyo closed Tue.** [BANKSY](https://thehyundaiseoul.ehyundai.com/culture/alt1) is optional **only if you drop Seochon** and accept a second cluster (Plan B/E). |
| **Nov 4** | Wed 🟨 | **[Deoksugung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) + Jeong-dong + [Sungnyemun](https://royal.khs.go.kr)** | **J3 walk from hotel.** Guard 11:00 → [Deoksugung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) to 21:00 → ⭐ **[MMCA Deoksugung](https://www.mmca.go.kr) Lee Daewon retrospective** (ends Nov 8; free Wed 18:00–21:00 or ₩2,000 daytime) → Jeongdong Observatory → **Seosomun Shrine History Museum** (Wed to **20:30**) → **SeMA Seosomun** (GanaArt to Nov 22) → [Sungnyemun](https://royal.khs.go.kr) Pasu + Namdaemun. |
| **Nov 5** | Thu 🟥 | **[Seonjeongneung](https://royaltombs.cha.go.kr/multiBbz/selectMultiBbzView.do?id=61&no=61&bbzId=tombs&mn=RT_01_08_03) + Bongeunsa** | **G1.** UNESCO tombs, free docent **10:00** (Nov–Jan 06:00–17:30, closed Mon, ₩1,000) → ⭐ **[Bongeunsa Temple Life](http://temple.bongeunsa.org/public_html/explain/temple_life.asp) (English) 14:00–16:00**, ₩30,000 → COEX Starfield as the indoor rest. Evening optional **[La Bohème](https://www.sejongpac.or.kr/)** at Sejong (walk from hotel after returning). |
| **Nov 6** | Fri 🟨 | **Namsan + [Namsangol](https://www.hanokmaeul.or.kr/ko/guide)** | **M2.** [Ahn Junggeun Memorial](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2013777) (Nov–Feb 10:00–17:00, closed Mon) → **Namsan Ormi** (free) → cable car → [N Seoul Tower](https://www.nseoultower.co.kr) → walk down to **[Namsangol](https://www.hanokmaeul.or.kr/ko/guide) Hanok Village** (**winter hours from Nov 1: 09:00–20:00**; interiors closed Mon). Lunch **[Hadongkwan](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949)** (open Fri) or **[Pildong Myeonok](https://english.visitseoul.net/eat/pildong-myeonok1/ENP013409)** (**Bib 2026**, closed Sun). |
| **Nov 7** | Sat 🟨 | **National Gugak Center** | **G3.** Free EN museum tour **14:00** + ⭐ **[토요명품](https://www.gugak.go.kr/site/program/performance/month_list?menuid=001001001&year=2026&month=11) 15:00** (₩20–30k). **Jongmyo** sits behind [Changdeokgung](https://www.cdg.go.kr) and is **closed Tue**; weekday visits are typically guided-tour-only and Saturday rules differ — **do not add it unless royal.khs.go.kr confirms a Nov 7 public slot** (⏳). Disability Arts exhibition at SAC Hangaram (Oct 30–Nov 15, free, closed Mon) is the rain indoor. |
| **Nov 8** | Sun 🟨 | **Changgyeonggung** | ⭐ **[Mulbit Yeonhwa](https://www.kh.or.kr/cms/content/view/1526) — final full 8-scene night** (from 16:40, ₩1,000, no booking, entry to 20:00, grounds 21:00, rain rule ≥3 mm at 13:00 cancels scenes 2 & 5). Pair with **Naksan / Ihwa** in the same Hyehwa walk (J5). MMCA Lee Daewon, History Museum expo, and SeMA Seoseoul **Moles** also **end today** — only add one if you skip the palace night. |
| **Nov 9** | Mon 🟩 | **[MMCA Seoul](https://www.mmca.go.kr) or [Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) encore** | Both **open Mondays**. Check-out 12:00. **National Hangeul Museum is ⛔ closed until Oct 2028** — not an option. |

## Optional (book 5 days ahead — do not lock the day on it)

**Han River History Tour** — ✅ Apr 20–Nov 15, 2026; slots 10:00–12:00 and 14:00–16:00; free; reserve at visit-hangang.seoul.kr **≥5 days ahead**; 5–15 people per run. Fits a Hangang cluster (Ichon / Yeouido) **if** a group is already forming. *(KoreaFun seoul #4)*
