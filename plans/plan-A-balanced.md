# 🗓️ Plan A — Balanced First-Timers (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** alternate busy and easy days; one district cluster per day; the two date-locked essentials (**BANKSY by Nov 3**, **Mulbit Yeonhwa by Nov 8**) are protected. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

| Date | Day / pace | Cluster | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nov 1** | Sun 🟩 | Myeong-dong | **Arrival (M1).** ⚠️ Marathon road closures until ~14:00 — subway only. Check-in 15:00 → cathedral → shopping streets + street-food stalls → dinner **Myeongdong Kyoja**/**Yeongyang Center** → **NANTA 17:00 or 20:00** (Sun shows 14:00/17:00). Optional: Mulbit Yeonhwa first taste (open Sun; from 16:40). |
| **Nov 2** | Mon 🟥 | Jongno | **Palace day (J1).** Gyeongbokgung guard ceremony 10:00 + palace + Folk/ Palace museums (open Mon) → lunch **Hwangsaengga Kalguksu** (Bib 2026) → Bukchon + Baek Inje House → Insadong + Ssamziegil → dinner **Songam Onban**/**Imun Seolnongtang** → evening: **Ikseon-dong cafés (J4)** or **DDP Dream in Light 19:00/20:00 (D1)** — both walkable/transit-light on a Monday when museums shut. |
| **Nov 3** | Tue 🟥 | Jongno → Yeouido | **Huwon + BANKSY deadline.** Morning: **Changdeokgung + Huwon Secret Garden** (open Tue; Huwon slot booked 6 days out at 10:00 on cdg.go.kr) → lunch **Ohsaegyehyang** or **Sadong Myeonok** → Seochon stroll (Tongin Market ⚠️ closed Mon but **open Tue**; Dilkusha) → ≈25 min taxi/subway to Yeouido → ⭐ **BANKSY: Still Here — LAST DAY** (The Hyundai Seoul ALT.1; Mon–Thu last entry **19:00**; prebooked ₩23,000) → Han riverside walk at Yeouido Park → home on Line 2? (transfer) or taxi. |

| **Nov 5** | Thu 🟥 | Gangnam | **Temple + COEX + opera (G1).** **Seonjeongneung** docent tour 10:00 → lunch **Heukdonga Gangnam**/**Budnamujip** → ⭐ **Bongeunsa Temple Life (EN) 14:00–16:00** (₩30,000) → **COEX: Starfield Library + Food Week Korea** (₩10,000) → dinner **Mapo Sutbulgalbi**/**Yangmani** → if booked: ⭐ **La Bohème** at Sejong Center (Nov 5–8; Gwanghwamun) or Seoul Arts Center **Glass Menagerie** Thu 19:30 / **SAC The NEXT oboe recital** 19:30 (₩20–40k). |
| **Nov 6** | Fri 🟨 | Hongdae | **West-side day (H1).** Late start → Line 2 direct → Gyeongui Line Forest Park, Yeonnam → lunch **Masichaina**/**Busanjib Hongdae** → cafés (Layered/Parole & Langue open Fri) → busking zones from afternoon → KBBQ dinner **MGM** (to 00:00)/**Eongteori** → **Starfield open-stage Fri 19:00** alternative at COEX if you skipped it Thursday → Line 2 home. Swap-in for foliage: H2 morning (Mangwon + Haneul silver grass) then Hongdae evening. |
| **Nov 7** | Sat 🟥⚡ | Seocho + night event | **Choice day.** Morning free / Garosu-gil ginkgo (G2, Sat-friendly) → ⭐ **Gugak Museum free EN tour 14:00 + 토요명품 15:00** (₩20–30k, Seocho) → **pick ONE night headline:** 🏆 **KGMA Day 1** (Gocheok) · 🎼 **Jujutsu Kaisen in Concert 18:30** (Kyung Hee Univ.) · ⚽ **Seoul E-Land 16:30** (Mokdong) · 🎤 **Regallily** (Hongdae, ₩88,000) — see clash table below. |
| **Nov 8** | Sun 🟨 | Jongno night | **Finals day.** Relaxed brunch (Moowee Nakwon opens 11:30 Sun — closes 16:00 Sun) → **KGMA Day 2** (time TBA — ⚠️ if it runs into the evening it collides with the palace; then pick KGMA *or* Mulbit) or **Jujutsu Kaisen matinee 14:00** (ends ~16:20 — compatible) or **La Bohème final performance** → early dinner near Hyehwa/Jongno → ⭐ **Changgyeonggung Mulbit Yeonhwa — FINAL full-8-scene night** (from 16:40; enter well before 20:00; ₩1,000; rain rule: ≥3 mm forecast at 13:00 cancels the two water scenes). |
| **Nov 9** | Mon 🟩 | Myeong-dong | **Check-out 12:00.** Morning: last Myeong-dong souvenirs / **Gyeongbokgung** (open Mon — the palace encore if Nov 2 rained) or **MMCA Seoul / National Museum** (both open Mondays; NMK Mon 09:30–17:30). Airport: AREX from Seoul Station (allow ≥3 h before an international flight). |
