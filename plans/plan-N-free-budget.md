# 🆓 Plan N — Free & Budget-Forward (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Prefer 🔎 free official sites and ✅ free timed programs. Paid items are optional upgrades only (BANKSY, Huwon, NANTA, shows). Mulbit at ₩1,000 is the planned splurge finale. Still closure-aware and one cluster/day. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** none required · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 3, 5, 7, 9
