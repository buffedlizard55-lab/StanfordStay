# 🏨 Plan K — Minimal Transit (Hotel Footprint) (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Maximize days with **0 subway rides** from Stanford Hotel Myeongdong. Only two planned rides: Tue BANKSY (Yeouido) and Sun Mulbit (Hyehwa). Everything else is a walk cluster off the hotel door. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** none forced — Nov 3/4 moderate · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 5, 7, 9
