# 🚶 Plan E — Walk-Cluster (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Rule:** commute **once** from the hotel to the day's neighbourhood (or walk from the door). After that, **no public transit between activities**. Return subway/taxi only at the end of the day. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

| Date | Day / pace | Cluster (stay inside it) | Commute from hotel | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nov 1** | Sun 🟩 | **Myeong-dong** | **0** — hotel door | **M1.** [Marathon](https://en.marathon.jtbc.com/) AM — subway-only if you arrive before ~14:00. Check-in 15:00 → cathedral → streets/stalls → **Kyoja / Yeongyang** → **[NANTA](https://www.nanta.co.kr/kr/show/detail.php?id=1) 17:00**. Optional **[Cheonggyecheon](https://cheonggyecheon.seoul.go.kr)** (3 min). |
| **Nov 2** | Mon 🟥 | **[Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) → [Bukchon](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr) → [Insadong](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/1270460)** | One ride: Line 3 to [Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) (~20 min) | **J1.** 10:00 guard ceremony (open Mon) → palace + Folk/Palace museums (open Mon) → walk to **Hwangsaengga** → [Bukchon](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr) alleys + **Baek Inje** (open Tue–Sun — skip house if you prefer not to cut Mon) → [Insadong](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/1270460) + [Ssamziegil](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/1270460) → dinner **[Songam Onban](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/SongamOnban/ENPhrnovc) / Imun**. **Do not add DDP** tonight — that is a second cluster. |
| **Nov 3** | Tue 🟨 | **Yeouido only** | One ride to Yeouido (~25 min) | ⭐ **[BANKSY](https://thehyundaiseoul.ehyundai.com/culture/alt1): Still Here — LAST DAY** at [The Hyundai Seoul](https://thehyundaiseoul.ehyundai.com/culture/alt1) ALT.1 (Mon–Thu last entry **19:00**, ₩23,000, prebook). Fill the rest of the day **inside Yeouido**: park walk + mall food hall. **Do not hop to Sebitseom or DDP** — those are other clusters (see X4, held). |
| **Nov 4** | Wed 🟨 | **City Hall / Jeong-dong / Namdaemun** | **0–8 min walk** from hotel | **J3.** [Deoksugung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) guard **11:00** → palace (to 21:00) + **MMCA Lee Daewon** (ends Nov 8) → Jeongdong-gil → **SeMA Seosomun** → [Sungnyemun](https://royal.khs.go.kr) Pasu + [Namdaemun Market](https://www.namdaemunmarket.co.kr) → **[Seoullo](https://seoullo7017.seoul.go.kr) 7017** home. Lunch **Goryeo Samgyetang** or **[Hadongkwan](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949)** (open Wed). |
| **Nov 5** | Thu 🟥 | **Samseong triangle: [Seonjeongneung](https://royaltombs.cha.go.kr/multiBbz/selectMultiBbzView.do?id=61&no=61&bbzId=tombs&mn=RT_01_08_03) ↔ Bongeunsa ↔ COEX** | One ride: Line 2 to Samseong/Seolleung (~30 min) | **G1 stay-put.** [Seonjeongneung](https://royaltombs.cha.go.kr/multiBbz/selectMultiBbzView.do?id=61&no=61&bbzId=tombs&mn=RT_01_08_03) docent 10:00 → lunch **[Heukdonga Gangnam](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Heukdonga-EN/ENP006289)** → ⭐ **[Bongeunsa Temple Life](http://temple.bongeunsa.org/public_html/explain/temple_life.asp) 14:00–16:00** (EN, ₩30,000) → [Starfield Library](https://starfield.co.kr/m/coexmall/starfieldLibrary/library.do) + **[Food Week](https://www.foodweek.co.kr/)** (₩10,000). **Opera is not in this triangle.** If you booked **[La Bohème](https://www.sejongpac.or.kr/)**, ride **home** first (hotel is the Gwanghwamun/Euljiro side), then **walk** to [Sejong Center](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/culture/cultureEvent/view.do?cultcode=146300&menuNo=200009). |
| **Nov 6** | Fri 🟨 | **[Changdeokgung](https://www.cdg.go.kr) → Seochon → Gwanghwamun** | One ride: Line 3 to Anguk (~20 min) | **J2 on Friday-late.** [Changdeokgung](https://www.cdg.go.kr) (open Fri; [Huwon](https://www.cdg.go.kr) if you booked) → Tongin + [Dilkusha](https://museum.seoul.go.kr/www/intro/annexIntro/annex_dilkusha/annex_dilkusha_01.jsp) → **[Seoul Museum of History](https://museum.seoul.go.kr) to 21:00** (Urban Planning expo **ends Nov 8**) + **[Sejong Story](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/culture/cultureEvent/view.do?cultcode=146300&menuNo=200009) Fri to 21:00**. Dinner **[Chanyang-jip](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=100177)** (open Fri) or Seochon **Daeha** (Mon–Sat 16:00–22:00). |
| **Nov 7** | Sat 🟥 | **Hannam / Itaewon** | One ride to Hangangjin (~25 min) | **I1 stay-put.** ⭐ **[Leeum](https://www.leeumhoam.org)** both shows (Inside Other Spaces to Nov 29 + Koo Jeong A to Dec 27; **closed Mon**) → **[Passion 5](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Passion-52/ENP013534)** → Gyeongridan **The Baker's Table** → Antique Furniture Street → dinner **Nariuijip** → ⭐ **[Elisabeth](https://tickets.interpark.com/contents/notice/detail/14348)** Sat **19:00** ([Blue Square](https://www.bluesquare.kr/), 10-min walk). Gugak/KGMA are other clusters — pick this **or** Plan A Saturday, not both. |
| **Nov 8** | Sun 🟨 | **Hyehwa / Changgyeonggung** | One ride: Line 4 Myeongdong → Hyehwa (direct) | **J5.** Ihwa + Naksan wall sunset → **[Hakrim Dabang](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Hakrim-Coffee-Shop/ENP025377)** → ⭐ **[Mulbit Yeonhwa](https://www.kh.or.kr/cms/content/view/1526) FINAL** (enter well before 20:00, ₩1,000). Brunch **[Moowee Nakwon](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/2024-mwnw/ENPp5wevo)** only if you start in Ikseon and **walk** north — do not subway between brunch and the palace. |
| **Nov 9** | Mon 🟩 | **Myeong-dong** | **0** | Check-out 12:00. **[Hadongkwan](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) 07:00** or last souvenirs. AREX from Seoul Station (~12 min walk via [Seoullo](https://seoullo7017.seoul.go.kr)). |

## Intentionally not combined (would break the rule)

- Changdeokgung **+** BANKSY on Tue Nov 3 (Jongno → Yeouido).
- COEX **+** Sejong opera without going home first.
- Leeum **+** N Seoul Tower (X6) — that is two clusters.
- Yeouido **+** Sebitseom **+** DDP (X4) — two subway hops.
