# 🚶 Plan E — Walk-Cluster (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Rule:** commute **once** from the hotel to the day's neighbourhood (or walk from the door). After that, **no public transit between activities**. Return subway/taxi only at the end of the day. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

| Date | Day / pace | Cluster (stay inside it) | Commute from hotel | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nov 1** | Sun 🟩 | **Myeong-dong** | **0** — hotel door | **M1.** Marathon AM — subway-only if you arrive before ~14:00. Check-in 15:00 → cathedral → streets/stalls → **Kyoja / Yeongyang** → **NANTA 17:00**. Optional **Cheonggyecheon** (3 min). |
| **Nov 2** | Mon 🟥 | **Gyeongbokgung → Bukchon → Insadong** | One ride: Line 3 to Gyeongbokgung (~20 min) | **J1.** 10:00 guard ceremony (open Mon) → palace + Folk/Palace museums (open Mon) → walk to **Hwangsaengga** → Bukchon alleys + **Baek Inje** (open Tue–Sun — skip house if you prefer not to cut Mon) → Insadong + Ssamziegil → dinner **Songam Onban / Imun**. **Do not add DDP** tonight — that is a second cluster. |
| **Nov 3** | Tue 🟨 | **Yeouido only** | One ride to Yeouido (~25 min) | ⭐ **BANKSY: Still Here — LAST DAY** at The Hyundai Seoul ALT.1 (Mon–Thu last entry **19:00**, ₩23,000, prebook). Fill the rest of the day **inside Yeouido**: park walk + mall food hall. **Do not hop to Sebitseom or DDP** — those are other clusters (see X4, held). |
| **Nov 4** | Wed 🟨 | **City Hall / Jeong-dong / Namdaemun** | **0–8 min walk** from hotel | **J3.** Deoksugung guard **11:00** → palace (to 21:00) + **MMCA Lee Daewon** (ends Nov 8) → Jeongdong-gil → **SeMA Seosomun** → Sungnyemun Pasu + Namdaemun Market → **Seoullo 7017** home. Lunch **Goryeo Samgyetang** or **Hadongkwan** (open Wed). |
| **Nov 5** | Thu 🟥 | **Samseong triangle: Seonjeongneung ↔ Bongeunsa ↔ COEX** | One ride: Line 2 to Samseong/Seolleung (~30 min) | **G1 stay-put.** Seonjeongneung docent 10:00 → lunch **Heukdonga Gangnam** → ⭐ **Bongeunsa Temple Life 14:00–16:00** (EN, ₩30,000) → Starfield Library + **Food Week** (₩10,000). **Opera is not in this triangle.** If you booked **La Bohème**, ride **home** first (hotel is the Gwanghwamun/Euljiro side), then **walk** to Sejong Center. |
| **Nov 6** | Fri 🟨 | **Changdeokgung → Seochon → Gwanghwamun** | One ride: Line 3 to Anguk (~20 min) | **J2 on Friday-late.** Changdeokgung (open Fri; Huwon if you booked) → Tongin + Dilkusha → **Seoul Museum of History to 21:00** (Urban Planning expo **ends Nov 8**) + **Sejong Story Fri to 21:00**. Dinner **Chanyang-jip** (open Fri) or Seochon **Daeha** (Mon–Sat 16:00–22:00). |
| **Nov 7** | Sat 🟥 | **Hannam / Itaewon** | One ride to Hangangjin (~25 min) | **I1 stay-put.** ⭐ **Leeum** both shows (Inside Other Spaces to Nov 29 + Koo Jeong A to Dec 27; **closed Mon**) → **Passion 5** → Gyeongridan **The Baker's Table** → Antique Furniture Street → dinner **Nariuijip** → ⭐ **Elisabeth** Sat **19:00** (Blue Square, 10-min walk). Gugak/KGMA are other clusters — pick this **or** Plan A Saturday, not both. |
| **Nov 8** | Sun 🟨 | **Hyehwa / Changgyeonggung** | One ride: Line 4 Myeongdong → Hyehwa (direct) | **J5.** Ihwa + Naksan wall sunset → **Hakrim Dabang** → ⭐ **Mulbit Yeonhwa FINAL** (enter well before 20:00, ₩1,000). Brunch **Moowee Nakwon** only if you start in Ikseon and **walk** north — do not subway between brunch and the palace. |
| **Nov 9** | Mon 🟩 | **Myeong-dong** | **0** | Check-out 12:00. **Hadongkwan 07:00** or last souvenirs. AREX from Seoul Station (~12 min walk via Seoullo). |

## Intentionally not combined (would break the rule)

- Changdeokgung **+** BANKSY on Tue Nov 3 (Jongno → Yeouido).
- COEX **+** Sejong opera without going home first.
- Leeum **+** N Seoul Tower (X6) — that is two clusters.
- Yeouido **+** Sebitseom **+** DDP (X4) — two subway hops.
