# 🧘 Plan Z — Slow Travel & Wellness (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** The gentlest plan in the whole system — one anchor per day, long unhurried blocks, green space every day, and only two "must-do" evenings (BANKSY, Mulbit) breaking the calm. Built for travelers who want to actually rest on a 9-day trip. Pace: mostly 🟩, with only one 🟨 day and zero 🟥 days.

**Busy days:** none (🟨 only on Nov 3 and Nov 8) · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9

## Why this plan has zero 🟥 days
Every other plan in this system schedules at least one full-cluster busy day; Plan Z intentionally caps every day at one or two anchors with long unstructured time between them, because a 9-day trip with a hard project deadline waiting at home (or simply low energy) benefits from **fewer decisions, not more sights**. The only fixed obligations are the two date-locked items that can't move (**BANKSY closes Nov 3**, **Mulbit's full run ends Nov 8**) — everything else is a suggestion, not a schedule.

## Wellness notes
1. **Free 24-hour, no-ticket green spaces used every day:** Namsan Outdoor Botanical Garden, Yeouido Park, Deoksugung Stonewall Walkway, Namsangol garden, Naksan Park — none require booking or timed entry.
2. **Bukchon Red Zone curfew (17:00–10:00)** is respected by keeping Nov 2's visit to the daytime window with no rush.
3. If Mulbit's rain rule cancels scenes 2 & 5 (≥3 mm forecast at 13:00 on Nov 8), the six remaining scenes plus the palace grounds are still worth the unhurried visit.
