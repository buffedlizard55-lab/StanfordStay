## Solo-travel logistics
1. **Seoul Guided Walking Tour** ([english.visitseoul.net/walking-tour](https://english.visitseoul.net/walking-tour)) runs **38 free courses** with volunteer guides in English/Japanese/Chinese/Korean — reserve online via [sto.or.kr](https://www.sto.or.kr/english/tourism01/view?stBusinessSeq=30), a good way to meet other travelers without booking a paid tour.
2. **T-money card** works solo without needing cash for every fare — buy at any convenience store.
3. Counter-seating restaurants (kalguksu shops, gimbap chains, kBBQ solo grills) are called out above; Korean dining culture is very solo-friendly for lunch, less so for late-night group-BBQ spots — those are used only for early dinners here.
4. **Mulbit Yeonhwa and BANKSY** are both busy public events by design — comfortable solo anchors rather than isolated ones.
