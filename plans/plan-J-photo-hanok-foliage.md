# 📸 Plan J — Photo, Hanok & Foliage (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Sequenced for November light and foliage: Bukchon/Gyeongbokgung, Huwon Secret Garden, Namsan, Haneul silver grass, Garosu-gil ginkgo, Naksan wall, Mulbit night media art. One photo cluster per day. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 2, 3, 6, 8 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 5, 9

| Date | Day / pace | Cluster (stay inside it) | Plan |
