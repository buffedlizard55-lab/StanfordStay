# 🌃 Plan Y — Nightlife & Late-Night Seoul (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Daytime is kept light so every evening can run late — night markets, 24-hour BBQ, late malls, and the two after-dark date-locked anchors (DDP Dream in Light, Mulbit Yeonhwa). Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy (mornings are deliberately soft after late nights).

**Busy days:** Nov 3, 6, 7 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 5, 9

## Late-night logistics
1. **Subway last trains run ~00:00–00:30** depending on the line — after that, taxi (Hongdae/Dongdaemun both have well-trafficked ranks).
2. **24-hour verified anchors used in this plan:** Minsokchon (KBBQ), Eunhangnamujip (marinated ribs), Doota Mall (to 00:00, extends to 05:00 Fri/Sat).
3. **Dongdaemun retail malls' closed days matter even at night** — Migliore closed Mon, Hello apM closed Tue — this plan places the Dongdaemun night crawl on **Tuesday** specifically so Migliore is open.
4. **BANKSY's last entry is 19:00** Mon–Thu — the Tue Nov 3 evening slot in this plan is cutting it close; arrive by 17:00–17:30 to be safe.
