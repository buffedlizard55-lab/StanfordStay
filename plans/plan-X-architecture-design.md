# 🏛️ Plan X — Architecture & Design (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** One building-focused cluster per day — a free architecture tour, a restored heritage structure, or a star-architect gallery — sequenced from colonial-era brick to Zaha Hadid curves. Distinct from Plan P (which contrasts "modern vs. palace" by theme): this plan is a straight architecture crawl regardless of era. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 4, 6 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 5, 9

| Date | Day / pace | Cluster (stay inside it) | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nov 1** | Sun 🟩 | Myeong-dong — Gothic Revival | ⚠️ [Marathon](https://en.marathon.jtbc.com/) closures AM. Check-in → **[Myeongdong Cathedral](https://www.mdsd.or.kr)** (1898, Korea's first Gothic-style brick cathedral) → dinner [Myeongdong Kyoja](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925) → [NANTA](https://www.nanta.co.kr/kr/show/detail.php?id=1) 17:00. |
| **Nov 2** | Mon 🟨 | Gyeongbokgung — Joseon royal architecture | [Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr) guard 10:00 + palace halls (Geunjeongjeon's dougong bracket roof, Gyeonghoeru's pond pavilion) — open Mon → lunch [Hwangsaengga Kalguksu](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=86236) → [Bukchon Hanok Village](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr) — 600-year-old hanok roofline (before 17:00 curfew). |
| **Nov 3** | Tue 🟨 | Seoul Station — old vs. new | Walk from hotel. **[Culture Station Seoul 284](https://parks.seoul.go.kr/template/sub/culturetank.do)** — restored 1925 Renaissance-dome former Seoul Station, Tue–Sun 10:00–19:00, closed Mon → **[Seoullo 7017](https://seoullo7017.seoul.go.kr)** elevated highway-turned-garden (opened 2017, adaptive-reuse landmark) — walk the full length. |
| **Nov 4** | Wed 🟥 | Ichon — National Museum's modern campus | One Line-4 ride. **[National Museum of Korea](https://www.museum.go.kr/MUSEUM/contents/M0101000000.do)** — one of Asia's largest museum buildings, free, open to 21:00 Wed → **[APMA (Amorepacific Museum of Art)](https://apma.amorepacific.com/visit/guide.do)** designed by David Chipperfield, closed Mon so Wed works. |
| **Nov 5** | Thu 🟩 | Myeong-dong side — restored heritage retail | **0 transit.** **[Shinsegae The Heritage](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/ShinsegaeTheHeritage/ENP6ptemj)** — restored 1930 former bank building, Mon–Thu 10:30–20:00 → [Bank of Korea Money Museum](https://www.bok.or.kr/museum/main/contents.do?menuNo=700112) free EN docent 14:00 (the museum building is itself a 1912 heritage structure) → [Namsan Outdoor Botanical Garden](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=80961) for a green-space break. |
| **Nov 6** | Fri 🟥 | DDP — Zaha Hadid's curves | Walk to DDP. ⭐ **[DDP Architecture Tour](http://www.ddp.or.kr/?menuno=782)** — free, 60 min, English run **13:30 daily Tue–Sun**, meet at the Museum 1F desk (book online, 1–8 people) → explore the building through dusk → ⭐ **[Dream in Light](https://culture.seoul.go.kr/culture/culture/cultureEvent/view.do?cultcode=156491&menuNo=200013)** façade projection show 18:00/19:00. |
| **Nov 7** | Sat 🟨 | Hannam — Leeum's three-architect museum | One ride Hangangjin. **[Leeum Museum of Art](https://www.leeumhoam.org)** — designed jointly by Mario Botta, Jean Nouvel, and Rem Koolhaas (three distinct wings) → [Blue Square](https://www.bluesquare.kr/) (converted warehouse-style performance hall) exterior walk. |
| **Nov 8** | Sun 🟨 | Changgyeonggung — palace architecture at night | Line 4 Hyehwa. [Naksan Park](https://parks.seoul.go.kr) city-wall walk (Joseon-era fortification architecture) → ⭐ **[Changgyeonggung Mulbit Yeonhwa](https://www.kh.or.kr/cms/content/view/1526) — FINAL night** — traditional palace buildings lit with contemporary media art, the clearest "old architecture meets new design" moment of the whole trip. |
| **Nov 9** | Mon 🟩 | Checkout — MMCA Seoul | **[MMCA Seoul](https://www.mmca.go.kr)** (Gwanghwamun branch, contemporary museum architecture, open Mon) or **[Gyeongbokgung](https://royal.khs.go.kr)** encore. Check-out 12:00. AREX via [Seoullo 7017](https://seoullo7017.seoul.go.kr). |

## Architecture-tour booking flags
1. **DDP Architecture Tour** is **free** — third-party listings quoting "₩10,000" are wrong; book the English 13:30 Tue–Sun slot online via ddp.or.kr, 1–8 people.
2. **Culture Station Seoul 284** is **closed Mondays** — this plan schedules it Tue.
3. **APMA is closed Mon** — paired here with Wed's National Museum late night.
4. **Leeum** is **closed Mon** — do not move Nov 7's Leeum visit to a Monday.
