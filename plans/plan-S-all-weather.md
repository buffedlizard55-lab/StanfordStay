# ☔ Plan S — All-Weather / Indoor-First (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Every day leads with an indoor anchor that survives rain, wind, or an early-November cold snap — museums, malls, underground shopping, department stores, and one big indoor amusement day (Lotte World). Outdoor stops are always the optional add-on, never the anchor. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 4, 7 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 5, 9

## Weather contingency notes
1. **Gyeongbokgung guard ceremony** is cancelled in heavy rain/snow — the palace interiors stay open regardless.
2. **Mulbit Yeonhwa rain rule:** ≥3 mm forecast at 13:00 cancels the two water-projection scenes (2 & 5) but the other 6 scenes and palace grounds still run — check the forecast at lunchtime on Nov 8.
3. **DDP Dream in Light** is an outdoor façade show — if it's raining, skip it in favor of the indoor DDP galleries (this plan doesn't schedule it as a headline for that reason).
4. **Namsan Cable Car / N Seoul Tower** are omitted from this plan by design (exposed cable-car ride) — swap in from Plan A/M2 on a confirmed clear day only.
