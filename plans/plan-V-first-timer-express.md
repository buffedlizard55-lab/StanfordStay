## Why this plan is "faster" than Plan A
Plan A alternates busy/easy days evenly across the whole week; Plan V front-loads five 🟥 days back-to-back (Nov 2, 3, 5, 6, 7) to cover the maximum number of iconic single-visit landmarks (Gyeongbokgung, Bukchon, Changdeokgung/Huwon, BANKSY, N Seoul Tower, Bongeunsa, Starfield Library, DDP, Hongdae, Mulbit) inside 9 days for a traveler who is unlikely to return soon and wants the checklist covered, accepting two very light bookend days (Nov 1, 4, 9) to recover.
