# 🌇 Plan T — Golden Hour & Viewpoints (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Sequenced around Seoul's early-November light — sunrise ≈07:10, sunset ≈17:20 — chasing free public viewpoints, skylines, and one sunrise anchor. Different focus from Plan J (foliage/hanok): this plan is about elevation and skyline, not gardens. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 3, 6, 8 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 4, 9

## Light-planning notes
- **Average sunrise ≈07:10–07:15, sunset ≈17:20–17:25** across Nov 1–9 (Seoul, lat 37.6°N) — golden hour runs roughly 30–60 min either side.
- **N Seoul Tower / Namsan Cable Car** and **Some Sevit** decks are the two reliable free/paid sunset-into-night anchors in this plan.
- **Mulbit Yeonhwa's rain rule** (≥3 mm forecast at 13:00 cancels scenes 2 & 5) matters here too — check before the Nov 8 finale.
- **Bukchon Red Zone tourist curfew (17:00–10:00)** means rooftop-line photography there has to happen **before** golden hour fully sets in during Nov — plan the Bukchon morning-light visit on Nov 2 instead of an evening one.
