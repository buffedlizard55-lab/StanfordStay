# 👨‍👩‍👧 Plan Q — Family & Kids (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** One family-friendly cluster per day, built around free/low-cost parks, hands-on museums, and one big kid-energy day (Children's Grand Park zoo + amusement rides). Closed-Monday children's facilities are flagged; Bukchon's 17:00 Red Zone curfew and Sunday restaurant closures are respected. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 4, 7 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 5, 9

## Family-specific booking flags
1. **National Museum Children's Museum** requires a **free but compulsory online reservation** — sessions run in 5 timed blocks (10:00–17:50), book via [museum.go.kr](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0103000000.do) up to 14 days ahead.
2. **Bukchon Red Zone rule:** tourist visits restricted **17:00–10:00 next day** (fine for violators since Mar 2025) — always leave the alleys by 17:00 with kids in tow anyway.
3. **Seoul Children's Grand Park** has **no weekly closed day** for the park/zoo — the safest all-ages anchor in this plan.
4. Namsan Cable Car and N Seoul Tower are **ticketed** (check current family pricing at [nseoultower.co.kr](https://www.nseoultower.co.kr) / [cablecar.co.kr](https://www.cablecar.co.kr)).
