# 💑 Plan L — Couple / Café & Evenings (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Slower pace, café anchors, one shared evening highlight most days (NANTA, Namsan, Elisabeth or La Bohème, Sevit, Mulbit). Still one walking cluster per day. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 5 only · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 6, 9

| Date | Day / pace | Cluster (stay inside it) | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nov 1** | Sun 🟩 | Myeong-dong easy arrival | Marathon AM subway-only. Check-in → [Myeongdong Cathedral](https://www.mdsd.or.kr) → hand-in-hand stalls → dinner [Myeongdong Kyoja](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?vcontsId=99925)/[Yeongyang Center](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Yeongyang-Center/ENP012371) → [NANTA](https://www.nanta.co.kr/kr/show/detail.php?id=1) 17:00 (fun, non-verbal) → [Cheonggyecheon](https://cheonggyecheon.seoul.go.kr) night lights. |

Early night. |

[Gyeongui Line Forest Park](https://parks.seoul.go.kr) → [Café Layered Yeonnam](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Caf%C3%A9%20Layered%20Yeonnam_/48856) + [Parole & Langue](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Parole-Langue/ENP0p4bkd) + [Antique Coffee Yeonnam](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/ANTIQUE-COFFEE/ENPfru4mc) → light [Hongdae busking](https://korean.visitkorea.or.kr/detail/ms_detail.do?cotid=c92fa510-548b-4da9-b6f4-7491c603bba6) → dinner [MGM Hongdae](https://english.visitseoul.net/MapoArea/2024-MGM/ENPtojamy)/[Masichaina](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=66922). Skip KGMA crush unless you both want it. |

| **Nov 9** | Mon 🟩 | Checkout breakfast | [Hadongkwan](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=192949) 07:00 together → check-out 12:00 → AREX. |
