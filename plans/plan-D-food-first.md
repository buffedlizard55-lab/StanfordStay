# 🍜 Plan D — Food-First (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** each day is built around one [Koreafood walking route](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/Koreafood/cities/walking-food-routes.html) (S1–S5) plus the nearest verified activity. **One neighbourhood per day.** No restaurant is named unless it is on the Koreafood verified roster (official hours). No restaurant prices. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

## Why this plan does not mix BANKSY or Temple Life into food days

Those two are real, verified, and date-locked — they live in Plans A/B/F. Adding Yeouido (BANKSY) or Samseong (Bongeunsa) onto a Jongno or Sindang food day would force a subway hop **between meals**, which this plan refuses.
