# 🙏 Plan W — Traditional Crafts & Temple Life (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** One temple, craft-museum, or hands-on heritage cluster per day, culminating in Bongeunsa's English Temple Life and the Mulbit night finale. Distinct from Plan F (which is palace-focused): this plan centers on Buddhism, craft, and hands-on tradition rather than royal architecture. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 3, 5, 7 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 4, 9

## Craft/temple booking flags
1. **Bongeunsa Temple Life** — every Thursday, English, ₩30,000; arrive at the foreigner information desk by 13:50 sharp.
2. **Museum Kimchikan** hands-on class fee is separate from the ₩5,000 base entry — confirm the class schedule on [kimchikan.com](http://www.kimchikan.com) before going.
3. **Gugak 토요명품** online booking closes 18:00 the day before; leftover seats sold at the box office 1 hour before curtain.
4. **Jogyesa** grounds are open 24 h/365 with no admission fee — the most flexible anchor in the whole plan, good as a rain-day fallback too.
