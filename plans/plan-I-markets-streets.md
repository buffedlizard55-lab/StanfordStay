# 🛒 Plan I — Markets & Streets (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Each day centers on one verified market or pedestrian street (Myeong-dong, Gwangjang/Ikseon, Tongin, Namdaemun, Dongdaemun, Mangwon, Folk Flea/Gyeongdong). Nearest activity only; no museum-hopping across the city. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 3, 6 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 4, 9
