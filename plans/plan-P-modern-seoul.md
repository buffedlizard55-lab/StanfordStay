# 🏙️ Plan P — Modern Seoul (Less Palace) (Nov 1–9, 2026)

**Shape:** Emphasizes contemporary culture: DDP Dream in Light, BANKSY, Leeum, COEX/Starfield/Food Week, APMA, Elisabeth, Garosu/Sevit. Palaces appear only as Mulbit’s media-art finale and optional tiny doses — not daily heritage marches. Pace: 🟥 busy · 🟨 moderate · 🟩 easy.

**Busy days:** Nov 3, 4, 5, 6 · **Easy days:** Nov 1, 2, 9
