## 🎤 Going to KGMA at Gocheok Sky Dome (Nov 7–8)

KGMA 2026 (3rd edition) is confirmed **Sat–Sun Nov 7–8 at Gocheok Sky Dome** — first time at Gocheok (moved from Inspire Arena; MCs Nam Ji-hyun, Woni, Kim Jae-won, Liz announced Jun 12). Lineup + tickets were TBA at the Aug 18 review — **watch kgma-is.com and buy the moment ticketing opens (~Oct)**. ⚠️ The KGMA site still carries Inspire-Arena shuttle text from the last edition — use **Gocheok** directions. *(KoreaFun seoul #5)*
- From the hotel: allow **≈45–60 min** each way (Line 1/7 routing via transfer; check Naver Map day-of; taxi ≈30–40 min).
- Pair a KGMA day with G3 (gugak 14:00–16:30) only if you accept a tight cross-town transfer — Seocho → Guro is the long way across town.
