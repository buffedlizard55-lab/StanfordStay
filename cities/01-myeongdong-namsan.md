# 🛍️ Myeong-dong + Namsan — Itineraries M1 & M2

**Cluster:** Myeong-dong (Jung-gu) + Namsan north side · **From hotel:** 0–10 min walk to everything in M1; M2 starts at Myeongdong Station (8-min walk).

---
